5. FFmpeg 9.0.2
---------------
Not installed with KUDUTEXT: downloaded the first time a video needs it (to put
subtitles into a video, or to read sound Windows can't), from Gyan's Windows
builds that ffmpeg.org links to, and checked against the SHA-256 checksum
written in KUDUTEXT. It's kept in %LocalAppData%\KUDUTEXT\FFmpeg with its own
licence (LICENSE.txt) and runs as a separate program.
This build is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3.
https://ffmpeg.org/
https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
